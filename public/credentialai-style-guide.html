Style Guide & Design System

The visual language behind Credentialai

Every token, component, and pattern documented here is pulled directly from the live product and case study — not a separate aspirational system. If it's here, it's actually in use.

Logo & branding

A dual-ring, always-rotating mark signals continuous verification rather than a one-time check — the two rings spin independently, in opposite directions, around a fixed glowing center.

Credentialai Primary lockup — mark + wordmark, "ai" in accent teal
Do

Keep the mark's two rings and center dot proportional and legible at any size — never crop the outer ring.

Don't

Recolor the mark, freeze its rotation in static contexts where motion is expected, or set the wordmark in any typeface but Space Grotesk.

Do

Always render "ai" in --accent (teal) against the "Credential" portion in --heading.

Don't

Place the mark on a light background — it's designed for the dark canvas and loses contrast on white.

Color

A dark, near-black canvas with a single teal-to-blue gradient carrying every signal that matters. Status colors (warn, danger) are reserved exclusively for verdict and validation states.
